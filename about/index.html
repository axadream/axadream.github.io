About

I am Alex Pintea, based in Transylvania, Romania. For more than twenty years I have worked in industrial IT, most of it as an MES consultant in automotive manufacturing. My daily work is writing technical requirement specifications: turning what production needs into precise, testable requirements that external developers implement in a production-tracking system, then checking the result against the specification.

In November 2026 I began a formal path into applied AI engineering, aiming at remote work on retrieval-augmented generation, agent workflows, API integration and small self-hosted models. I am at level 1 of 6 in that path and publish every step: a daily log, an open study handbook, and projects built with AI coding tools where each entry states who wrote the code.

My education is a master's in applied informatics and a bachelor's in environmental engineering. Outside AI I practise Blender and Three.js.

What you will find here: the OpenHorizon app (a shipped, installable PWA), the practice log with proof links, and the AI Engineering Handbook. What you will not find yet: production LLM experience. The log shows the pace at which that gap closes.
